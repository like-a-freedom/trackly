## Direction contract
THESIS: The route is the working object. One map and one task pane replace duplicated competing editor panels.
OWN-WORLD: White working surfaces, cool neutral canvas, dark ink, blue actions, restrained borders and system typography. Icons identify actions; geography supplies the visual content.
STORY: Draw or import, describe, review, save and share. Optional advanced operations stay available in context. Recorded measurements remain attached to their original geometry.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop reserves a 360px right task pane beside the map; mobile keeps map tools above a pane with collapsed, medium and full states. Save and Back remain in the top bar.
